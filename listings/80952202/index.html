De slaapkamers bevinden zich op de tweede verdieping en de volledige leefruimte is gesitueerd op de verdieping erboven. Dankzij deze indeling geniet de woonkamer van veel natuurlijk licht, privacy en een vrij uitzicht. Vanuit de keuken geven openslaande deuren toegang tot het ruime terras. De woning heeft energielabel A, de erfpacht is eeuwigdurend afgekocht en een snelle oplevering is mogelijk. Een comfortabele woning voor wie ruim en rustig wil wonen met het stadscentrum binnen handbereik. Located in the green and spacious residential neighbourhood of De Aker, this generous maisonette offers a private entrance, three bedrooms and approximately 116 m² of living space. The bedrooms are situated on the second floor, with the entire living area located on the floor above. Thanks to this layout, the living room benefits from plenty of natural light, privacy and open views. French doors from the kitchen provide access to the spacious terrace. The property has an energy label A, the ground lease has been bought out in perpetuity, and a quick completion is possible. A comfortable home for those looking for spacious and peaceful living while keeping Amsterdam city centre within easy reach. Location Vätternkade is located in De Aker, a quiet and spacious residential neighbourhood in Amsterdam Nieuw-West, characterised by plenty of greenery, water and wide streets. The immediate area offers a variety of shops, schools, childcare facilities, sports amenities and playgrounds. For everyday groceries, De Dukaat shopping centre is nearby, while Osdorpplein offers a wider range of shops and services just a short distance away. Sloterplas lake and Sloterpark are also close by. Accessibility is excellent. Several tram and bus connections provide convenient links to Lelylaan Station and the rest of Amsterdam. By car, the A10 ring road and the A4, A5 and A9 motorways can all be reached quickly. Schiphol Airport, Haarlem, Hoofddorp and Amstelveen are also easily accessible. Layout Ground floor Private entrance with hallway and stairs leading to the residential floors. Second floor The central hallway provides access to the three bedrooms, the bathroom and a storage room with a connection for the washing machine. The bedrooms are all well proportioned and feature large windows. The largest bedroom has a spacious walk-in wardrobe, offering plenty of room for clothing and additional storage. The bathroom is fitted with a generous walk-in shower, washbasin with vanity unit and toilet. Third floor The entire living area is located on the top floor. The spacious living room at the front features a wide expanse of windows and offers ample space for a comfortable seating area and a large dining table. Its top-floor position provides plenty of natural light and privacy. The open-plan kitchen at the rear features a modern black-and-white colour scheme and offers generous worktop and storage space. The breakfast bar/dining area creates a natural connection with the living room. French doors open onto the generous roof terrace of approximately 13 m², with ample space for a dining table, lounge area and plants. From the terrace, you can enjoy wide, open views across the surrounding area. This floor also features a separate toilet and additional storage space. Storage The property includes a separate external storage unit of approximately 7 m², ideal for bicycles and additional storage.
