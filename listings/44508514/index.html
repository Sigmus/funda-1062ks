Description

Move-in ready 60 m² apartment (NEN-measured) in a characterful building in Amsterdam’s historic city centre. Stylish living space with a sleek open kitchen, a roof terrace, and a well-finished interior. All city-centre amenities are within easy reach. Layout: Shared entrance with access to the apartment on the third floor. The cosy living room with open kitchen sits at the rear and opens onto the roof terrace. The modern open kitchen has a timeless design and built-in appliances. At the front are two bedrooms that can easily be combined into one, or removed entirely for a larger living floor. The upper floor has two bedrooms and a bathroom. The modern bathroom has a walk-in shower and washbasin; there is also a separate toilet in the hallway. The finish makes the apartment move-in ready. Location: Gasthuismolensteeg is in the historic heart of Amsterdam, a short walk from Dam Square, Kalverstraat and the Nine Streets. Shops, restaurants, cafés and culture are nearby, with excellent public transport links.
