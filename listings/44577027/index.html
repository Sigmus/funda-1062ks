Description

NIEUW! #Prime Waterfront Location#|Living by the Water# Outdoor Living Situated in an exceptional location directly on the Ringvaart, this remarkable semi-detached dike house offers a unique combination of waterfront living, comfort and outdoor life. Because the property is built on the dike, it benefits from an additional living floor in the basement. This level has been converted into a fully independent living space, making the property suitable for multi-generational living or dual occupancy. The house is built on a concrete pile foundation and is well and sustainably insulated. It is fully gas-free and equipped with a heat pump. Despite its original construction date of 1950, the property has an impressive energy label A+. Outdoor Living The property truly comes into its own outdoors. It features a wide and deep south-west-facing rear garden, with several fruit trees, plenty of greenery and a detached garage. Directly behind the living room is a spacious south-west-facing terrace, ideal for enjoying long summer evenings dining, having drinks and relaxing with family and friends. There is private parking next to the house, while free street parking is also available directly in front of the property. A particularly special feature is the private jetty on the Ringvaart, which is included in full ownership. It can be used for mooring your own boat, fishing, or simply as an additional waterside terrace. From the Ringvaart, you can sail directly towards Amsterdam, reaching the Amsterdam canals by boat in a relatively short time. On warm summer days, local residents also enjoy swimming in the water. Location Badhoevedorp combines the peace and friendly atmosphere of a village with excellent accessibility. Thanks to its location close to Amsterdam, Schiphol Airport and major motorways such as the A4, A5 and A9, Amsterdam and the surrounding cities and towns are easily accessible. Public transport also provides convenient connections to various destinations. Badhoevedorp itself offers a wide range of amenities, including shops, supermarkets, restaurants and cafés, primary schools and various sports facilities. For an even wider range of shopping, dining and cultural options, Amsterdam, Amstelveen and Hoofddorp are all within easy reach. Here, you can enjoy the peace and privacy of living directly on the water, while the amenities and energy of the city are just a short distance away. Layout Ground Floor Entrance hall with access to the meter cupboard and separate toilet. From the hallway, there is access to both the basement and the upper floors. The spacious living room benefits from large windows and French doors opening onto the sunny terrace. Adjacent is the modern kitchen, renovated in 2024 and equipped with various built-in appliances. First Floor Landing providing access to the various rooms, a separate toilet, infrared sauna and bathroom with bathtub, shower and washbasin. Originally, this floor contained two bedrooms; it is currently arranged as one spacious master bedroom. Attic Floor Spacious bedroom with Velux skylights. Basement The basement has been converted into a fully independent living accommodation with air conditioning. It features French doors, a generous living area with sleeping section, its own kitchen and a bathroom with shower, washbasin and toilet. This space is ideal for dual occupancy, elderly care, guests, an au pair or a live-in family member. Outdoor Space The property boasts a wide, deep and sunny south-west-facing rear garden, complete with fruit trees. At the rear of the garden is a detached garage. The property's private jetty is located directly on the Ringvaart, providing a unique opportunity to enjoy the water right from your own home.
