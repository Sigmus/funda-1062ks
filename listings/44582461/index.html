Very solidly built and excellently maintained semi-detached home with a spacious driveway offering room for no fewer than three cars, one of which can be parked comfortably under the practical carport. And two under cover if you count the garage! This lovely home certainly leaves scope for a new owner to make some changes to suit their own taste, but the fundamentals are simply very good: solid, well cared for and beautifully maintained. A truly lovely home in a great location in Badhoevedorp! The house currently has three generously sized bedrooms. Creating a fourth bedroom is easy by simply reinstating a wall, while adding an extra dormer window at the front could even make a fifth bedroom possible. The exceptionally wide, street-facing living room is something a little different and gives the house a wonderful sense of space. The kitchen at the rear directly adjoins the lovely, private garden. A great layout that connects indoor and outdoor living beautifully. The bathroom also stands out for its generous proportions. Everything you need is there, yet there is still plenty of room to move around. And lots of natural light too. Very nice indeed. The garden measures approximately 55 m² and enjoys the afternoon and evening sun. Wonderfully spacious, yet still easy to maintain. The surrounding greenery provides plenty of privacy as well as welcome shade and cooling on warmer days. The spacious garage of approximately 19 m² is extremely practical and can be accessed from both the driveway and the rear garden. Ideal for a car, bicycles, storage or, of course, as a hobby space. Directly in front of the garage is the super-practical carport. Perfect for getting in and out of the car in the dry, unloading the shopping or simply keeping your car sheltered right outside your home. The entire property is fitted with solid window frames and insulating glazing. The ground floor has a concrete floor with underfloor heating. Also good to know: there is no increased foundation risk. And then there is the location. Torricellistraat is a very pleasant and spacious residential street, with plenty of distance between the houses, creating an open and comfortable feel. You can enjoy peaceful, green surroundings here while the centre of Badhoevedorp, with its shops, restaurants and everyday amenities, is within easy walking distance. Badhoevedorp itself is also extremely centrally located: Amsterdam is nearby, as are Schiphol Airport, Amstelveen and the main arterial roads. Peaceful living, without having to give up the convenience of the city. The key facts: Living area approximately 128 m², garage approximately 19 m², plot size 228 m² and built in 1968. The house is solidly constructed with brick façades. Asking price: € 700.000,- costs payable by the buyer (k.k.).
