Description

Located at Pieter Calandlaan 463, this spacious home features a practical layout, an impressive 150 m² of living space, four bedrooms, and a private garden. Recently renovated, the property boasts a modern, well-maintained appearance. Thanks to its generous living areas and smart design, the home offers plenty of possibilities for large families, working from home, and pursuing hobbies. The house is spacious and features a dual-aspect living room with an extension; large windows at both the front and rear ensure an abundance of natural light. The practical layout creates a pleasant flow between the various living areas. With four bedrooms, the home offers ample space for the whole family as well as other potential uses. Additionally, there is a spacious garden at the rear, easily accessible via a convenient back entrance. Layout Ground floor: The home has a separate entrance leading into a central hallway. From here, you can access the various ground-floor rooms, including the separate toilet, living room, and kitchen. The bright living room offers plenty of space for a generous seating area and flows seamlessly into the dining room. The modern, updated kitchen connects beautifully to this space and is fully equipped. A passageway from the dining room leads to a practical utility room, which provides access to the neat, low-maintenance back garden with rear access. First floor: The first floor houses three bedrooms, two of which feature built-in wardrobes for extra storage. Also located here is the spacious, recently modernized bathroom, which includes a double vanity, a walk-in shower with a rain showerhead, and a second toilet. Second floor: The second floor features a very spacious landing with a washbasin, offering plenty of potential to create, for example, an additional bathroom or a laundry room. Also located here is the fourth bedroom; thanks to the dormer window, it feels spacious and bright, providing a pleasant, quiet retreat. Land status: The property is situated on leasehold land owned by the Municipality of Amsterdam (perpetual leasehold system). The ground rent is indexed annually; the current fee is relatively low at €558 per year, and the current lease period runs until May 1, 2068. Ground rent payments are tax-deductible. Surroundings: The property is located on Pieter Calandlaan in Amsterdam Nieuw-West, a lively area with various shops, supermarkets, schools, and sports facilities nearby. Sloterpark and Sloterplas are easily accessible for relaxation and recreation. There are also various restaurants and cafés in the vicinity. Accessibility is excellent. There are various tram and bus connections in the immediate area, and the A4, A5, A9, and A10 motorways are easily accessible by car. In summary: - Large family home with a garden, offering no less than 150 m² of living space. - 4 spacious bedrooms. - Recently renovated with a sleek finish. - Modern bathroom with double vanity and walk-in rain shower. - Modern kitchen with appliances. - Low-maintenance garden with back access. - Conveniently located in Amsterdam Nieuw-West. - Good accessibility by public transport and car. With 150 m² of living space, four bedrooms, and a generous garden, this is a home offering plenty of space and possibilities. A lovely family home that you can furnish to your own taste and where you can live comfortably for years to come.
