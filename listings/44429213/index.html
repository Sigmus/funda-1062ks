Spacious Four-Room Apartment on the Tenth Floor with Storage Unit
A spacious four-room apartment located on the tenth floor, including a private storage unit on the ground floor. The building is equipped with an elevator.
The property is situated in Amsterdam Nieuw-West. Within just a few minutes, you can reach Osdorpplein, which features a large shopping center with various restaurants. Osdorpplein is also home to Theater De Meervaart, where you can enjoy outstanding performances. In addition, De Meervaart offers talent development programs for children and young people.
For recreation, you can visit the nearby Sloterplas lake, or simply relax on your own southeast-facing balcony.
The A9 and A5 motorways can be reached within a few minutes by car.
