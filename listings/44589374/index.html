LAYOUT (see also the interactive floor plans and alternative layout) The property is accessed via its own private entrance on the ground floor. Immediately to the left is the first bedroom. With an alternative layout, this room could be incorporated into the living area, creating a spacious through-living room with an open-plan kitchen. Centrally located within the property are the meter cupboard with an upgraded electrical distribution board and a separate toilet. The living room and kitchen adjoin the garden directly, which can be accessed through sliding doors. At the rear there is also a communal back passage, allowing easy access to the storage room by bicycle. The first floor comprises three well-proportioned bedrooms. There is also a separate storage room housing the central heating system and mechanical ventilation, with space for a washing machine and dryer. Finally, this floor features a bathroom with a washbasin unit and shower enclosure. OWNERS’ ASSOCIATION (VvE) The property forms part of an Owners’ Association (VvE) comprising 30 apartment rights. Van Abbestraat 18 is apartment index 9, with a 100/2961th share in the communal property. The VvE is responsible for the management and maintenance of the communal parts (excluding window frames), the building insurance, and the communal footpaths and access roads. This ground rent will therefore only become payable from 1 October 2045 and, under current regulations, is tax-deductible in most cases.
