Aanvaarding in overleg. ----------------- ATTENTION HOME SHARERS! On a quiet side street off the Rozengracht, in the heart of the beloved Jordaan district, you will find this spacious apartment near the Westertoren. What makes this property particularly unique is that it comes with a permit for four non-self-contained residential units! The apartment is situated on freehold land and forms part of a small-scale complex consisting of a commercial space on the ground floor and a total of five apartments above. The foundation was renewed in 2009. A truly unique location! Not only are there various shops in the immediate vicinity, but also a wide selection of charming restaurants and cafés. The popular Saturday market on the Lindengracht is within walking distance. The Anne Frank House and the beautiful Westerkerk can be reached within minutes, as well as Westerpark, Vondelpark and Museumplein. In addition, schools, daycare centres and sports facilities are all located nearby. Accessibility is excellent as well, with tram, bus, metro and train connections (including Amsterdam Centraal) close at hand. Whether you are looking for an apartment to share with others or a spacious family home, this is a unique opportunity! Layout: Shared staircase with the apartment entrance located on the fourth floor. Upon entering, you step directly into the inviting living room with open-plan kitchen. The kitchen is equipped with a fridge/freezer combination, dishwasher, oven, 5-burner gas stove and a boiling water tap (Quooker). There is also a utility cupboard housing the washing machine connection and the central heating boiler. From the living room, there is access to the spacious bathroom, fitted with a washbasin cabinet, toilet and walk-in shower. Next to the bathroom is one of the four bedrooms. The other three bedrooms are connected to the generous hallway, which also features a practical built-in storage cupboard.
