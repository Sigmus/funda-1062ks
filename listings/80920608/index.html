Living surrounded by greenery, with all amenities close at hand! Located in the sought-after Buitenveldert neighbourhood, on the second floor, this spacious, ready to move in, bright four-room corner apartment offers no less than 86 m² of living space. Featuring a sunny south-facing balcony, modern finishes, a spacious private storage room and a permanent leasehold that has been fully bought off, this property offers everything you need for comfortable and carefree living. The ideal combination of peace, space and a central location makes this apartment a wonderful place to call home. Location The apartment is situated in a prime location close to the Zuidas business district, with all essential amenities and major roads within easy reach. The property is just a short distance from the A10 motorway, Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, Amsterdam UMC – VUmc and the RAI convention centre. Despite its urban location, the area offers plenty of greenery. Nearby you will find Amstelpark, Gijsbrecht van Aemstelpark and ’t Loopveld. The Amsterdamse Bos is also within walking distance, ideal for a walk, sporting activities or simply enjoying a relaxing afternoon surrounded by nature. In terms of accessibility, this location is particularly well connected. Amsterdam Zuid Station is just a short distance away and offers excellent train and metro connections. In addition, the tram stop is only a 2-minute walk away, providing convenient access to the surrounding area. From Amsterdam Zuid Station, Schiphol Airport can be reached in approximately 10 minutes. The A10 motorway is also quick and easy to access. For your daily groceries, you do not have to go far. A small shopping centre, including Jumbo and Albert Heijn, is within walking distance, while the popular Gelderlandplein shopping centre is practically around the corner for more extensive shopping. In short: an ideal location for those who want to combine the energy and amenities of the city with peace, greenery and excellent accessibility. Layout You enter the building through the communal entrance, equipped with an intercom system and mailboxes. The second floor can be reached via a well-maintained staircase. The apartment has an entrance hall with a utility electric cupboard, which was completely renovated in 2019. All rooms are accessible from the hallway. The modern bathroom features a spacious walk-in shower with a Grohe mixer tap, a wide double washbasin and a designer radiator. There is also a toilet and a separate bidet. The bathroom has mechanical ventilation and underfloor heating. The separate toilet room has a toilet and washbasin and is equipped with mechanical ventilation. There are three bedrooms, all of which are quietly situated on the north side of the building, helping to maintain a pleasant and comfortable indoor temperature during the warmer summer months. The master bedroom and a second bedroom both have direct access to the enclosed balcony (loggia), creating a light and versatile extension of the living space. The second bedroom is also ideally suited for use as a home office, children’s bedroom or guest room. The very spacious and bright living room provides access to a south-facing balcony. From the balcony, you overlook the green communal courtyard and its playground. The modern L-shaped kitchen is fitted with a range of Bosch built-in appliances, including a refrigerator, separate freezer, combination microwave oven, five-burner gas hob with wok burner, dishwasher and recirculating extractor hood. A spacious private storage room of approximately 9 m² is located on the ground floor.
