De master bedroom bevindt zich aan het einde van de hal en biedt voldoende ruimte voor een comfortabel tweepersoonsbed en een ruime kast. LAYOUT The well maintained communal entrance provides access to the apartment by both stairs and lift. On the fourth floor, the private entrance opens into a central hallway, providing access to all rooms. Thanks to the corner position and numerous windows, the apartment enjoys an abundance of natural daylight and a wonderfully spacious feel. The bright living area offers plenty of room for both a comfortable lounge and dining area. The kitchen has been thoughtfully designed with a playful layout and a central island creating a lovely social space and the perfect spot to enjoy your morning coffee. The kitchen is fitted with a range of Siemens and BORA built-in appliances, including a fridge-freezer combination, induction hob with integrated extractor, sink, oven and dishwasher. There is also plenty of cupboard space for all your kitchen essentials. From the living room, you step directly onto the sunny west-facing balcony of approximately 12 m². A wonderful outdoor space to enjoy the afternoon and evening sun. The apartment features three bedrooms. The spacious master bedroom is located at the end of the hallway and comfortably accommodates a double bed and a large wardrobe. The two additional bedrooms are also bright and versatile, thanks to their large windows. They are ideal as children's bedrooms, guest rooms, home offices or hobby spaces. The spacious bathroom is finished in light, neutral tones and features a walk-in shower and washbasin. The separate toilet is fitted with a small washbasin. The hallway also provides access to a practical internal storage/laundry room with connections for a washing machine and dryer. On the ground floor, the property benefits from a private parking space in the secure parking garage, complete with its own EV charging point. Four private bicycle spaces are also available in the communal bicycle storage. FUTURE CANAL One of the property's particularly interesting future features is the planned development of the Nieuwe Osdorpergracht. The environmental permit has been granted and construction is expected to start in early 2027. Once completed, the canal will create a new waterside setting and provide the apartment with a future view over the water. We would be happy to provide further information about the plans upon request and can show you what the future situation is expected to look like. An augmented reality (AR) link is also available upon request, allowing you to experience an impression of the future canal. More information about the development of the Nieuwe Osdorpergracht and the Stadscentrum Osdorpplein can be found via the Municipality of Amsterdam. LOCATION The apartment is located in Amsterdam Nieuw-West, close to a wide range of green spaces, recreational facilities and everyday amenities. Sloterpark and Sloterplas are just a short distance away and are perfect for a morning run, a walk or a relaxing afternoon outdoors. On warm summer days, the city beach at Sloterplas is a popular spot for swimming and enjoying the sun. The nearby Osdorper Binnenpolder offers a more peaceful, rural setting and can easily be reached by bicycle. Everyday shopping is particularly convenient, with four supermarkets within walking distance: Albert Heijn XL, Lidl, Dirk and Jumbo. You will also find a wide variety of shops nearby, including HEMA, H&M, Etos, Douglas and Action. There is plenty to enjoy in the area when it comes to culture and entertainment. Theater De Meervaart is located nearby, while the new Filmhuis & Verhalenhuis is also planned within walking distance, with the opening currently scheduled for the fourth quarter of 2027. The apartment is very well connected to the rest of Amsterdam and beyond. Several tram and bus routes are within easy reach, taking you to Amsterdam city centre in approximately 15 minutes. The Vondelpark is around 15 minutes away by bicycle. By car, the A4, A5, A9 and A10 motorways are easily accessible, providing convenient connections to Haarlem, Schiphol Airport and The Hague. Amsterdam Nieuw-West has seen significant development in recent years, with new residential areas, amenities, cafés and restaurants, as well as improvements to public spaces and infrastructure.
