Layout Through the porch and the outdoor staircase, you reach the apartment’s private entrance on the first floor. You enter the hallway, which provides access to the various rooms of the apartment. At the front is the bright living room with an open kitchen. It is decorated in neutral colors and equipped with various built-in appliances, such as an induction cooktop, combination oven, fridge-freezer, dishwasher, and washer-dryer combo. Next to the living room is the first bedroom. This room is also located at the front of the apartment and works well as a bedroom, but can also serve as a home office or study. At the rear are two bedrooms. The master bedroom features French doors leading to the balcony, and the balcony is also accessible from the other bedroom. Thanks to the view of the courtyard gardens at the rear, both rooms have a peaceful atmosphere. The balcony faces west, allowing you to enjoy the sun here in the afternoon and evening. Located in the center of the apartment is the modern bathroom, equipped with a bathtub, walk-in shower, vanity, and towel radiator. The apartment also features a separate toilet with a hand-washing sink. Neighborhood Located in a prime spot in the popular De Baarsjes neighborhood!
