Dit goed onderhouden appartement ligt midden in De Pijp in het marktvrije, rustige, tramvrije gedeelte van de Albert Cuypstraat. De woning beschikt over een lichte, riante woonkamer met half open keuken gescheiden door een bar welke het centrum van de ruimte is. Location & accessibility You will live right in the heart of De Pijp, just steps away from numerous trendy cafés, bars and restaurants. Within a few minutes, you can walk to Sarphatipark, pick up fresh produce at the Albert Cuyp Market, or easily cycle towards the city centre and Oud-Zuid. The apartment is a short distance from De Pijp metro station on the North/South Line and the Albert Cuyp parking garage. There is currently a waiting list of approximately 9 months for a residents' parking permit in De Pijp. Amsterdam RAI, Zuid and Amstel railway stations are also easily accessible. In short, this is a wonderful location for those who want the vibrancy and diversity of Amsterdam within easy reach, while enjoying peace, natural light and a generous south-facing outdoor space at home. The hallway provides access to all rooms: the bright living room with semi-open kitchen, three bedrooms, the bathroom and the separate toilet with washbasin. The spacious living room of approximately 22 m² features a large window façade with opening windows, allowing plenty of natural daylight to enter. There is ample space for both a full-size dining table and a comfortable seating area. The semi-open kitchen features a breakfast bar and a direct view into the living room. The kitchen is equipped with a combination oven, four burner gas hob, dishwasher and a separate American-style double-door refrigerator. The apartment has three bedrooms at the quiet rear of the property. The balcony can be accessed from one of the bedrooms. The smaller bedroom is also ideally suited as a home office, study or children's room. The bathroom features a walk-in shower, vanity unit and connections for a washing machine and dryer. The separate toilet with washbasin is located directly next to the bathroom. At the rear is a sunny south-facing balcony of approximately 7 m².
