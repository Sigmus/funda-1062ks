At James Rosskade in Amsterdam-West, InKamers brings these wishes together. This bright apartment of approximately 96 m² will be fully furnished for four students, with four bedrooms, a shared living room and two balconies. A comfortable base from which to begin student life in Amsterdam. **Living together, with space of your own** Cooking together after lectures, catching up over dinner or relaxing during a film night. The shared living room provides a setting for the everyday moments that make living with housemates enjoyable. At the same time, each student has a bedroom in which to unwind, study and recharge. The apartment is on the third floor and will be furnished with attention to comfort, atmosphere and practicality. The kitchen, bathroom and separate toilet are shared. Key features: - Approximately 96 m² of living space for the entire apartment. - Four bedrooms and a shared living room. - Two balconies. - Delivered fully furnished in accordance with the InKamers concept. - Ground rent has been paid off in perpetuity. - A conversion permit for four residential units with shared facilities. - A mortgage cannot be secured against a co-ownership share purchased under the InKamers concept. **A practical way to support your child** With InKamers, you purchase a 25% co-ownership share in the apartment. This allows you to contribute financially to your son’s or daughter’s accommodation while sharing ownership with three other co-owners. The rooms are not linked to individual ownership shares. As the property manager, InKamers allocates the rooms, enabling students to switch rooms within the apartment. InKamers arranges the tenancy agreements based on the individual arrangements. InKamers also continues to manage the property, allowing you to leave the day-to-day management in professional hands. The services, costs and responsibilities are set out in the management agreement. As a co-owner, you retain the obligations associated with ownership. **Everyday life in Amsterdam-West** James Rosskade is situated in a part of Amsterdam where city life meets green spaces.
