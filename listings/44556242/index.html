SPACIOUS FAMILY HOME WITH 4 BEDROOMS AND A 14-METRE-DEEP GARDEN ENJOYING THE AFTERNOON AND EVENING SUN Situated in a highly desirable residential street near the centre of Badhoevedorp What a wonderful home! This bright and spacious family residence offers everything you would expect from a comfortable family home. Featuring four bedrooms, a deep rear garden enjoying the afternoon and evening sun, and an excellent location close to all daily amenities, this is a property where the next owners can enjoy many years of comfortable living. The floor plans can be found throughout the presentation and at the end of the brochure, but of course we would be delighted to show you the property in person. One of the first things you'll notice is the generous size of the home compared to similar properties. The excellent width and depth create a spacious feel throughout. In addition, the house offers great potential for a rear extension, ideal for buyers looking to create even more living space. The kitchen is located at the rear overlooking the garden. While fully functional, it would benefit from modernization, offering an excellent opportunity to create your own contemporary open-plan kitchen. Opening up the kitchen to the living room is also a possibility. The bathroom was thoughtfully positioned on the first floor by the previous owners and is fully tiled, featuring a bathtub, separate shower, washbasin, and a second toilet. In 2013, the property was partially renovated and one of the things were the second floor was enhanced with a dormer window and a fully insulated roof. This created a beautiful and fully functional additional floor with plenty of natural light and space, ideal as an extra bedroom, home office, or hobby room. Following the installation of a second dormer window, this level could also accommodate two separate bedrooms. The interior and exterior painting have been carried out recently, and the front gutter has also been replaced. Location The property is situated in a quiet residential street within walking distance of the centre of Badhoevedorp. Amenities such as the Albert Heijn supermarket, various shops, cafés, and restaurants are all nearby. Several primary schools, including the Rietveldschool and Oranje Nassauschool, are also within easy walking distance. An ideal location for families seeking comfortable living with everything close by.
