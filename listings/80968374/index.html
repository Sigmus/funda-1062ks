VAN WOUSTRAAT 216-1 | AMSTERDAM BRIGHT AND CHARMING TWO LEVEL APARTMENT OF APPROX. 90 M² WITH THREE BEDROOMS, THE POTENTIAL FOR A FOURTH AND TWO BALCONIES IN DE PIJP Located on Van Woustraat, this bright and charming apartment offers approximately 90 m² of living space across two floors. The property combines a spacious living area with an open plan kitchen, three bedrooms, the potential to create a fourth bedroom and two balconies overlooking the rear of the building. Wooden flooring, a neutral colour palette and large windows create a warm and inviting atmosphere throughout. The living and dining areas flow naturally into one another, while the open plan kitchen with its central island provides a wonderful space for cooking and entertaining. Both balconies offer a pleasant outdoor retreat, allowing you to enjoy the fresh air on either floor. LAYOUT The entrance leads into the bright living room at the front of the property. Large windows allow natural light to flood the space and offer views over Van Woustraat. There is ample room for a comfortable seating area. The adjoining dining room benefits from windows on two sides, creating a bright and inviting space of its own. The open plan kitchen is situated at the rear and features white cabinetry, a dark worktop and a central island with seating. An ideal setting for starting the day with a cup of coffee or enjoying a leisurely breakfast. French doors open onto the first balcony, where you can relax and enjoy the evening sun. This floor also accommodates a bedroom and a separate toilet. The staircase leads to the upper floor. UPPER FLOOR The upper floor comprises two bedrooms and a bathroom. One of the bedrooms features French doors opening onto the second balcony at the rear of the property. The bathroom is fitted with a bathtub, washbasin and toilet. With three bedrooms and the potential to create a fourth, the apartment offers considerable flexibility. Whether you are looking for a family home, a dedicated home office or a guest bedroom, the layout can be adapted to suit your individual needs. LOCATION Van Woustraat is situated in the heart of De Pijp, one of Amsterdam's most vibrant and sought after neighbourhoods, renowned for its diverse selection of shops, speciality stores, cafés and restaurants. Everyday amenities are right on your doorstep, while the famous Albert Cuyp Market and Sarphatipark are just a short walk away. Whether you are enjoying a coffee at one of the neighbourhood's many cafés, taking a stroll through the park or dining at one of the excellent local restaurants, Amsterdam's vibrant city life is quite literally at your doorstep. ENERGY LABEL The property has an energy label C LEASEHOLD The property is situated on municipal leasehold land. The current leasehold period runs until 31 May 2046. The annual ground rent amounts to €726.73. The property is subject to a continuing leasehold arrangement. HOMEOWNERS' ASSOCIATION The Homeowners' Association (VvE) consists of three apartment rights and is managed by the owners themselves. The apartment represents a 37.5% share in the association. Major maintenance work was carried out on the building in 2020. This included the renovation of all three balconies at the rear, the installation of an entirely new roof covering on the flat roof and the replacement of the lead flashing around the chimneys. The pointing along the roof edges was also repaired where necessary. At the time, the owners agreed to carry out essential maintenance as required rather than building up a structural maintenance reserve. Consequently, no monthly service charges are currently payable, and no long term maintenance plan (MJOP) has yet been established. The current administrator will consult with the new owner regarding the preparation of a long term maintenance plan and the introduction of a maintenance contribution. FOUNDATION RISK The building has a foundation risk indication within categories A, B or C. These categories indicate a very low to moderate risk of foundation related issues.
