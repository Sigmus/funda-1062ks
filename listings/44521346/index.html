Situated on the third and top floor of an apartment complex in Bos en Lommer, this attractive apartment offers approximately 63 m² of living space. The current owner largely modernised the property in 2026, including the installation of a new open-plan kitchen, an elegant herringbone floor and a new central heating system. The bright rooms, soft colours and tasteful finishes create a warm and comfortable home. With three bedrooms, two balconies facing east and west, and a private storage unit on the ground floor, the apartment offers a practical layout and plenty of possibilities. A wonderful place for those seeking peaceful living while keeping all the amenities of the city within easy reach. Layout The communal entrance and staircase lead to the apartment on the third floor. The hallway provides access to the various rooms. At the front of the apartment is the bright living room, where the large windows provide plenty of natural light. The attractive herringbone floor and warm finishes give the room a welcoming atmosphere. From the living room, you can step out onto the west-facing balcony: a lovely place to enjoy the afternoon and evening sun. The open-plan kitchen, renovated in 2026, connects seamlessly with the living room. It features calm, light-coloured cabinetry, a dark worktop and new built-in appliances. A boiling-water tap has also been installed. There is space for a dining table by the window, making this the perfect setting for cooking, dining and spending time together. The second balcony can be accessed from the kitchen and faces east, making it ideal for enjoying a cup of coffee in the morning sun. Two bedrooms are situated at the rear of the apartment and can also be used as a home office, children’s room or guest room. The third bedroom is located at the front and houses the central heating boiler and a connection for a washing machine. The bathroom is fitted with a shower, washbasin and washing machine connection. The separate toilet can be accessed from the hallway. A private storage unit of approximately 7 m² is located on the ground floor, providing practical space for bicycles and additional storage.
